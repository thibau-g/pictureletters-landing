About Us

At the dinner table, in November 2025, Mum said, “I don’t want flowers at my funeral. They’re expensive, bad for the environment, and go to waste afterwards. Instead, I want happy memories that make people smile. I want photos of me and my loved ones on letters that spell MUM.”

Mum (Claudie) is creative. And I (Thibau) am entrepreneurial. So starting a business together was always going to make sense. And having just started the process of selling a small venture I had launched a year earlier, I figured this idea had legs.

So started months of research and development, which led to what you see today. And since the initial idea, we realised that collages on MDF shapes goes far beyond funerals. Numbers for Birthdays. Names for Weddings. Initials and words for around the house. Locations for Holidays. We can help everybody display their memories in amazing ways!

Picture Letters will one day service Mum’s funeral. And probably mine too. And I hope yours and your loved ones. But before that inevitable day, Picture Letters will also have a place on everybody’s wall to display your favourite memories on a shape that means something to you.

Captured memories, treasured forever.
